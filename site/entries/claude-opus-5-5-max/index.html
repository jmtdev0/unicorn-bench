What is a unicorn?

One horn, a thousand stories

A unicorn is a creature with a single horn on its forehead. That is the whole definition, and somehow it has been enough to fill four and a half millennia of art, science, faith and fantasy.

It has been a wild ass in India, a fearsome monster in Rome, a symbol of purity in medieval Europe, an omen of wise rulers in China, the national animal of Scotland and, lately, a billion-dollar startup. Real animals, from rhinos to narwhals, kept the legend alive, and travelers kept getting the details gloriously wrong.

Six true facts about an imaginary animal

Unicorns may not exist, but their paper trail is very real.

Scotland's national animal is a unicorn

Unicorns have guarded the Scottish royal arms since the Middle Ages. Since 1603 the royal coat of arms has paired the English lion with a Scottish unicorn, which is traditionally shown in chains.

Why the chains?

Renaissance "unicorn horns" were narwhal tusks

Traded down from the Arctic, the spiral tusks sold for more than their weight in gold. Kings drank from them, hoping to foil poisoners.

A real "Siberian unicorn" met early humans

The Indian rhino is officially a unicorn

Its scientific name, given by Carl Linnaeus in 1758, is Rhinoceros unicornis: the "one-horned nose-horn".

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Asked by humans, answered as honestly as a unicorn website can manage.

Are unicorns real?

No living unicorn has ever been documented by science. But the legend is very real, and so are the animals that fed it: the Indian rhinoceros, the narwhal, the oryx and the extinct "Siberian unicorn", Elasmotherium. You can meet all of them in the Bestiary.

Why is the Scottish unicorn wearing a chain?

Heraldic unicorns are traditionally shown chained. The usual explanation is that a free unicorn was considered the wildest and proudest of beasts, so a chained one showed the power of the king who could tame it.

What is the difference between a unicorn, a pegasus and an alicorn?

A unicorn has a horn. A pegasus has wings, after Pegasus, the winged horse of Greek myth. An alicorn originally meant the unicorn's horn itself (from the Italian alicorno); calling a winged unicorn an alicorn is a modern fantasy usage. In the Atelier you can build either kind.

What is a unicorn horn made of?

In legend, a substance that neutralizes poison. In the treasure chambers of Renaissance Europe, ivory from narwhal tusks, which are teeth. A rhinoceros horn, by contrast, is made of keratin, the same material as your fingernails.

What do unicorns eat?

Medieval bestiaries are silent on the matter; they were more interested in how to catch one. Modern sources overwhelmingly suggest rainbows and marshmallows, a theory that remains un-peer-reviewed.

Why are billion-dollar startups called unicorns?

Venture capitalist Aileen Lee coined the term in 2013 for privately held startups valued at over a billion dollars, because back then they were about as rare as the real thing.

Is there a day for unicorns?

In the United States, April 9 is celebrated as National Unicorn Day. In Scotland, of course, every day is unicorn day.
